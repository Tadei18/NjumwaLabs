## The problem

Edge Tech Gurus offers AI automation, web development, and advertising on monthly retainers. It needed two connected things: a marketing site that sells those service lines, and a client portal where customers create accounts, choose plans, and track ongoing work — a complete platform rather than a static site.

## The approach

I built the marketing site — service lines, an interactive automation-flow diagram, subscription plans and retainer tiers, and a scoping-call flow — and a separate client portal as a web app where clients create accounts, track projects, work with specialists, and are billed monthly. The two hand off cleanly: "client login / create account" on the marketing site opens the portal.

## The stack

Astro for the fast marketing front end; a Next.js client portal with authentication, project tracking, and account-based access (encrypted in transit); monthly billing by card or PayPal.

## The result

A full agency platform — a marketing front end that converts and a working client portal behind it — demonstrating both marketing-site and full-stack web-app delivery. (The portal sits behind a login; an anonymised authenticated screenshot can be added if desired.)
