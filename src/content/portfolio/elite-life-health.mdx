## The problem

Elite Life Health Services provides RN-supervised, non-medical home care to seniors and adults recovering from illness in the Dallas area. They needed a trustworthy, compassionate website to reach families and generate free-assessment enquiries, with clear local presence in Addison, Texas.

## The approach

I built a multi-page marketing site — personal care, companion care, and dementia/memory care services; an RN-supervision "why choose us" story; a simple "how it works"; testimonials; an in-action gallery; and clear enquiry paths (free assessment, call, WhatsApp) — optimised for local search in the Dallas/Addison area.

## The stack

A responsive HTML/CSS/JavaScript site with local SEO for the Addison/Dallas service area, plus WhatsApp and click-to-call enquiry.

## The result

A warm, credible home-care site that gives families clear next steps and supports local discovery in its Texas service area — and a demonstration of delivering for a US client.
