## The problem

Essay Writing Edge needed far more than a marketing site: a working order-and-payment platform spanning academic writing, programming, research, tutoring, and automation — each service with its own fields and pricing logic — with account creation and secure payment built in.

## The approach

I built a dynamic, multi-service order form that adapts its fields to the service selected, calculates the exact price live (by level, deadline, and complexity), creates a client account, supports file uploads, and takes payment through Stripe — including a pay-in-full or 50%-deposit option — with orders and messages living in a portal. Clear educational-use framing is built into the flow before payment.

## The stack

A dynamic, multi-step order/checkout system with a live pricing engine, Stripe payments (full or deposit), account creation and a client portal, and file uploads.

## The result

A complete order-and-checkout platform for a service business — dynamic pricing, secure payments, and account management in one flow — demonstrating full-stack delivery well beyond a static site.
