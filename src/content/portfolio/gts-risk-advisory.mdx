## The problem

GTS Risk Advisory delivers intelligence-driven security, risk consulting, and operational resilience to corporations, governments, and individuals across 12+ African markets. They needed a corporate presence that communicated a broad, sophisticated service catalogue to boards and executives — credible and easy to navigate, not a template.

## The approach

I built a large, structured corporate site organised under a clear mega-menu: 12+ operational and specialist service lines, a consulting practice area, industries served, and an intelligence hub — including an interactive, Africa-wide "Risk Heat Map" — plus an insights/briefings section, careers, and contact. The tone and structure were designed for authority and clarity across dozens of pages.

## The stack

A multi-page architecture with a content-driven insights section and an interactive risk heat-map tool, fully responsive, with SEO and social metadata throughout.

## The result

A credible, intelligence-led corporate site that positions the firm for enterprise and government engagements and turns a wide service catalogue into a navigable, professional experience.
