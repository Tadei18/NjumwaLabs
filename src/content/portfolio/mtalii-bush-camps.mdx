## The problem

Mtalii Bush Camps is a boutique luxury tented retreat at the foothills of Mount Kenya in Nanyuki. As a premium hospitality brand, it needed a website that looked the part — showcasing tented suites, five dining venues, a spa, and event spaces — while loading fast for international travellers and turning interest into direct enquiries.

## The approach

I designed and built a multi-section marketing site: three tented "villages" of accommodation, five dining venues, activities, spa & wellness, events and conferencing, rates, a gallery, and travel guides — all in a warm, golden-hour visual language. Guests get clear paths to enquire through a booking enquiry flow, a WhatsApp concierge, and click-to-call, with mapping and directions built in.

## The stack

Built on Astro for a fast, static, SEO-friendly site, fully responsive across devices, with WhatsApp enquiry, Google Maps, and complete Open Graph / social metadata for sharing.

## The result

A polished, conversion-focused hospitality site that presents the camp at a genuine luxury standard, gives guests multiple low-friction ways to enquire, and loads quickly for an international audience.
